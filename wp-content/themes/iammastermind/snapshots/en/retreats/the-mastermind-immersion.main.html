6 Days in a Transformational Space.

The in-person retreat is an intimate, carefully held 6-day experience in one of our most pristine retreat sanctuary locations, strictly limited to a small group of up to 20 participants. This is where profound disruption meets unparalleled safety. You are not treated as a number; you are held as an individual navigating deeply meaningful inner work. The container is intentionally designed to help your nervous system settle and your awareness expand drastically.

  • Cognitive & Emotional Processing: Extensive guided self-awareness training, elite education, and structured reflection groups.

  • Physical Embodiment: Transform tension into flow with a 60-minute massage, targeted yoga sessions, deep release practices, somatic breathwork, and traditional ancestral baths.

  • Ancestral Immersion Ceremonies: Optional deep-state ceremonies held with absolute care, preparation, respect, and rigorous attention to each person’s readiness and wellbeing. These are never treated as isolated peak experiences, but as vital catalysts within a wider transformational process.

  • Premium Logistics: Retreat accommodation, all nutrient-dense meals, and roundtrip airport transportation are entirely handled so your only focus is your own evolution.

The true measure of a breakthrough is what survives the flight home. In many ways, the return is where the real work begins. Mastermind is not interested in giving you a powerful experience and abandoning you to figure out the rest.

  • The Mastermind Integration Blueprint: A custom plan to bridge the gap between jungle revelations and everyday reality.

  • 90 Days of Active Support: Two online integration circles, 90 days of structured online training, and ongoing group connection.

  • The Post-Journey Arsenal: A comprehensive kit providing guidance on how to navigate post-retreat shifts, how to stay grounded in your new baseline, and how to seamlessly apply your insights to your relationships, leadership, and daily choices.
